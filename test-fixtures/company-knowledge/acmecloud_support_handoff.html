Support levels

  • L2: product configuration and reproducible bugs.
  • L3: engineering deep-dives and code-level fixes.

Escalation criteria

L1 escalates to L2 when a ticket is unresolved for 1 business day or the issue reproduces on a clean environment. L2 escalates to L3 when a bug requires source access or the resolution would change documented behavior.

Handoff requirements

A handoff is only accepted when the ticket notes include a summary, the steps already tried, logs or reproduction steps, and the customer impact. Escalation targets: L1 to L2 within 1 business day; L2 to L3 within 2 business days; SEV-1 tickets escalate immediately to the Incident Manager.
