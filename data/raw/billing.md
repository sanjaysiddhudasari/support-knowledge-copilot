# Billing

## Overview
Billing in AcmeCloud is managed by the billing owner — the user responsible for the subscription, invoices, and payment method. Billing is separate from project administration; a billing owner may not be a project administrator.

## Plans
| Plan | Storage | Users | Key features |
|------|---------|-------|--------------|
| Free | 5 GB | 2 | Community support |
| Pro | 100 GB | 25 | Email support |
| Business | 1 TB | 250 | Priority support |
| Enterprise | Unlimited | Unlimited | SSO + SAML, dedicated support, custom rate limits |

Enterprise pricing is customized; contact sales for a quote.

## Invoices
Invoices are generated monthly and are available in Billing Settings → Invoices. The billing owner receives invoice notifications by email. Past invoices can be downloaded as PDF.

## Billing Owner
Each organization has one billing owner. Only the Owner (organization role) or an administrator can change the billing owner. The billing owner can be any organization member with the Owner or Admin role.

## Changing Plans
1. Open Billing Settings.
2. Select Change Plan.
3. Choose a plan.
4. Confirm.

Upgrading takes effect immediately; downgrading takes effect at the next billing cycle.

## Payment Methods
Payment is by credit card. The billing owner manages the payment method in Billing Settings. A failed payment results in an email notification and, after 14 days, a downgrade to the Free plan.

## Billing FAQ
- Who receives invoices? The billing owner.
- Can a project administrator change the plan? No; only the billing owner or an Owner/Admin with billing permission can change the plan.
