# Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

## Overview
Multi-factor authentication adds a second verification step to sign-in using a time-based one-time password (TOTP) generated by an authenticator app such as AcmeCloud Authenticator, Google Authenticator, or Authy. When MFA is enabled, signing in requires both your password and a six-digit code.

## Enabling MFA
1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Select Security, then Multi-Factor Authentication.
3. Select Enroll.
4. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app.
5. Enter the six-digit code shown in the app to confirm.
6. Save your backup codes.

## Disabling MFA
To disable MFA, open Security Settings and select Disable. You must provide your current password and a valid MFA code. If you no longer have access to your authenticator app, see Lost MFA Device below; do not attempt to disable MFA without it.

## Backup Codes
When you enable MFA, AcmeCloud generates 10 single-use backup codes. Each code can be used once to sign in when you do not have your authenticator app. Store the backup codes securely. Regenerating backup codes invalidates the previous set.

## Lost MFA Device
If you lose your device:
1. Sign in with your password and select "Use a backup code".
2. Enter one of your backup codes.
3. Enroll a new device.

If you have no backup codes, contact your organization administrator, who can reset your MFA from the Admin Console. Resetting MFA does not reset your password; the two are independent. See account-recovery.md.

## MFA Enforcement (require_mfa)
Organizations can require MFA for all users by enabling the require_mfa setting. When enabled, users who have not enrolled must do so within a 14-day grace period, after which they cannot sign in until MFA is enrolled. See security.md.

## MFA Troubleshooting
- Codes rejected: ensure the time on your device is set correctly; TOTP codes are time-sensitive.
- If you changed phones, use a backup code to enroll the new device.
- An MFA reset by an administrator does not affect your password or your files.
